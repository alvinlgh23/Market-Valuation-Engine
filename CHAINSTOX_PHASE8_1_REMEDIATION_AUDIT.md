## Disagreements and policy boundaries

- Stopping an unsupported easing inference is the user's requested integrity correction. It does **not** implement the audit's proposed relative demand-versus-capacity policy, which remains deferred.
- The FY2024 RPO value was independently verified in the FY2024 filing, rather than attributing it to an unverified comparative passage in FY2025. Primary source: [Palantir FY2024 Note 3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1321655/000132165525000022/R11.htm).
- Operating lease inclusion is not silently equated with a missing-borrowings defect. Existing debt-completeness gates remain. Missing Yahoo debt no longer defaults to zero. Documentary-zero attestation and lease-adjusted FCFF/WACC need policy approval.
- WACC, SBC, FCFF/FCFE, terminal value, growth fade, CapEx and Reverse DCF formulas were not changed. ADR conversion is withheld pending approved FX-date/source and share-denominator conventions.
