# Company Research Frontend Audit

## Scope

This audit covers the pre-refactor `frontend/` application and the Company Research experience only. Macro, inflation, fragility, historical, and watchlist modules remain outside the redesign.

## Before

- The root client page owned every module and switched views with local React state.
- Company Research was a hardcoded `CompanyView` using only `company-pltr.ts`.
- `CompanyReport` expected generic tables for business evidence, valuation, positioning, one indexed chart, thesis bullets, and data quality.
- `data-provider.ts` returned PLTR for every ticker and had no Company Economics contract.
- Navigation and command handling recognized `company PLTR`, but did not provide ticker routes.
- The company view reused `TerminalPanel`, `TerminalTable`, `EvidenceLedger`, `TriggerList`, and `TerminalCharts`, producing a dense panel/card layout.
- Company price/fundamentals chart values, valuation values, positioning, research summary, and source labels were manually authored frontend mock data.
- Macro-adjacent views contained explicit placeholder modules and mock status copy.

## Reused

- Existing Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind pipeline, and dark research palette.
- Root terminal shell, command palette, overview, fragility, watchlist, and other non-company modules.
- Terminal-inspired typography for identifiers, metrics, commands, and status labels.
- Restrained borders and the existing command-navigation concept.

## Replaced for Company Research

- The single hardcoded company view is superseded by `/company/[ticker]`.
- Generic terminal cards/tables are replaced with section hierarchy, structured rows, causal map nodes, expandable constraints, evidence filters, and a full report overlay.
- The old `CompanyReport` mock shape is not used by the new route.
- The permanent root sidebar and market watch rail are not shown inside Company Research because they compete with the research narrative.
- Decorative price/fundamental charting is not carried forward.

## Backend vs Previous Frontend Shape

The previous frontend shape contained presentation-specific arrays. The backend `company_economics` object instead contains identity, classification, growth architecture, typed evidence IDs, constraints, scaling metrics, sustainability, falsification conditions, financial translation, valuation reviews, market expectations, data gaps, a causal graph, and validation.

The new adapter centralizes:

- enum-to-human-label conversion;
- unavailable values;
- confidence and direction wording;
- evidence typing and section usage;
- graph and constraint decoration;
- numeric formatting;
- counts for evidence filters.

Archetype and constraint presentation metadata is exported directly from the backend registries into `ontology.json`; it is not recreated by ticker in React.

## Fixture and Integration Boundary

PLTR, NVDA, MSFT, JPM, COST, and TSM JSON fixtures are generated by `scripts/export_company_economics_fixtures.py` using the backend serializer. `FixtureProvider` implements the intended `CompanyResearchProvider` boundary. A future HTTP provider can replace the fixture lookup without changing research components.

Current fixture limitations are intentionally visible: no live market price, no frontend fair-value scenario range, one comparable financial period, and company-specific backend data gaps. The UI does not infer or fabricate those values.
